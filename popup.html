⚠ Homophones can't be auto-corrected — writing "their" vs "there" are both valid words. TypeEase can't know which you meant without reading the sentence. Knowing your weak spots helps.

Why ADHD spelling has a pattern

ADHD impairs the graphemic buffer — the working-memory store that holds letter sequences during writing. The errors aren't about not knowing how to spell. They're attention slipping mid-execution.

Algorithm

TypeEase uses Damerau-Levenshtein distance — the edit-distance measure that includes transpositions (swapped adjacent letters), which is the #1 ADHD error type. Built on a BK-tree for fast lookup. Seeded from the Birkbeck corpus (36,133 real misspelling pairs).
